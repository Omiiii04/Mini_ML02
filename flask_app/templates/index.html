Why CardioSense

Built for Clarity & Precision

Two ways to assess your risk. One powerful model underneath.

Manual Input

Enter your clinical parameters via our guided, validated form. Sliders, selectors and real-time range feedback make it effortless.

File Upload

Upload a CSV or text file containing your health data and get an instant prediction without filling in every field manually.

Instant Results

Results render in milliseconds with a clear risk percentage, a colour-coded verdict and actionable next-step guidance.

Privacy First

All data is processed locally in your session. Nothing is stored or transmitted to third parties. Your health data stays yours.

The Process

Three Steps to Clarity

  1. 01

    Enter Your Details

    Provide your age, blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG readings, and other clinical markers via the intuitive prediction form.

  2. 02

    Model Analysis

    Our KNN model — trained on 918 patient records — scales your inputs and finds the most similar cases to determine your risk profile.

  3. 03

    Get Your Score

    Receive a clear risk percentage, a Low / High verdict, and recommended next steps — all in under a second.
